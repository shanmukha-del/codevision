Guidelines

Official Codevision Rules

Ensure complete compliance with our technical and procedural guidelines for a fair, high-standard competition.

01

Core Web Standards

Websites must be developed using pure HTML5 and CSS3. Vanilla JavaScript may be used for interactions, but external frontend frameworks (React, Vue, etc.) are strictly prohibited.

02

Team Composition

Teams must consist of 2 to 3 members (Duo or Trio). All members must be registered college students from recognized universities or polytechnics.

03

Duration & Timings

Event duration is 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Participants must report by 9:30 AM for QR ticket check-in and system allotment.

04

Deadline Discipline

Participants must submit their final project archive before the 2:30 PM deadline. Late submissions will face score penalties.

05

Originality of Work

External pre-built website templates and copied site packs cannot be used. All markup and stylesheet structures must be written during the event.

06

Thematic Alignment

Teams must strictly follow their assigned theme. Projects misaligned with the problem scope will not qualify for top prizes.

07

Viva & Presentation

Participants must present their completed website on screen to the jury panel, explaining design choices, CSS architecture, and responsiveness.

08

Jury Final Authority

The decision of the faculty coordinators and jury members will be final and binding. Any malpractice or plagiarism leads to instant disqualification.
